GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered clearly.

When do I pay for a product?+

Only after DirectBelgium confirms availability and sends you a valid, itemised quotation. A reservation inquiry does not guarantee supplier inventory.

Are customs and clearing charges included?+

They depend on product classification, valuation and current regulations. Your quotation explains whether each estimate is included or excluded.

Can I book a consultation before choosing a product?+

Yes. Our team can discuss your needs and explain the process before you submit an inquiry.
